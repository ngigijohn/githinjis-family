About this project

A home for our family's story

This site began in 2020 as a small web-design project: a single page with a picture gallery and a hand-drawn family tree of John Ngigi's immediate family. It has grown into a shared archive for the whole extended family, so we can trace our genealogy, record the people who came before us and keep those memories for the generations to come.

How it works

Every person has a profile. Couples are recorded as unions, and children are linked to their parents, which lets the site draw the tree automatically and work out any relationship, however distant.

Contributing

Family members can request an editor account. Once an administrator approves it, you can add relatives, dates, life events, stories and photos.

Privacy

Birth dates of living relatives are only visible to signed-in family members. If you'd like something about you changed or removed, let us know.
