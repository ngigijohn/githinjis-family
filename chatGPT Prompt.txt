# Build a Modern Family Tree and Ancestry Web Application

You are a senior product designer, UX designer and full-stack engineer.

I want you to BUILD a polished, production-quality family tree / ancestry web application. Do not just give me an explanation, wireframe, or list of ideas. Actually create the application in the provided coding environment.

The goal is to create a genealogy product that feels significantly more modern, visual and enjoyable than a traditional genealogy database.

Think of the useful functionality found in products such as Ancestry and MyHeritage, but create our own original UI, branding and user experience. Do not copy their visual designs.

---

# 1. PRODUCT VISION

The application allows a person or family to build a rich digital history of their family.

The family tree is the central experience, but the product should go beyond boxes connected by lines.

Users should be able to:

* Build and explore their family tree
* Create detailed profiles for relatives
* Upload photographs and documents
* Record family stories and memories
* See family members on a timeline
* See where ancestors lived on a map
* Calculate relationships between people
* Collaborate with relatives
* Preserve historical information
* Discover connections within their family history

The application should feel like:

"Google Maps + a beautiful digital scrapbook + an interactive family tree."

It should feel warm, trustworthy, sophisticated and slightly emotional.

Avoid making it look like an old enterprise database.

---

# 2. DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a beautiful modern interface.

Design principles:

* Clean
* Elegant
* Warm
* Highly visual
* Easy to understand
* Family-oriented
* Trustworthy
* Responsive
* Accessible
* Fast

Use generous spacing and excellent typography.

Use cards, subtle borders, soft shadows and restrained animations.

The family tree should be the visual centerpiece.

Do not overcrowd the interface.

The UI should feel premium without looking unnecessarily complicated.

Support both light and dark mode.

Use a restrained natural/warm color palette.

Possible visual inspiration:

* archival photographs
* old paper
* maps
* family albums
* genealogy documents
* modern SaaS dashboards

But keep the implementation contemporary.
